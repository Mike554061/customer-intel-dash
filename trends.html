How a new customer is identified — the Email Anchor. Every order-confirmation email that lands in depot@supplynow.org is parsed and its customer email is captured.
  1. Capture the customer email from the confirmation and treat it as the Master ID for that account.
  2. Query the full order history for that exact email (normalized) — an immediate duplicate check.
  3. No match → first-time order: it's a new customer, dated to this order.
  4. Match found → returning: the order attaches to the existing account, and its cadence/trend updates.
Everything on this page is computed live off that email-anchored order history — no manual tagging.
